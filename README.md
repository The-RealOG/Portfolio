# Portfolio
A modern personal portfolio showcasing my interdisciplinary expertise across computer science, robotics, and biomedical research at Northwestern University. Built with HTML5/CSS3 featuring elegant glass morphism effects, responsive design, and subtle animations. The site highlights my teaching experience as a CS 110 TA, backend development work for Daily Northwestern, robotics research at the Center for Robotics & Biosystems, and biomedical research contributions. The clean, contemporary interface with neon accents creates an engaging user experience while maintaining professional presentation of my academic background, technical projects, and diverse skill set. View live: [https://the-realog.github.io/Portfolio/]
